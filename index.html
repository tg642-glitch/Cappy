Twenty fictional test invoices, separate from the race weekend, go through the same live pipeline as a review: the Team agent classifies them with the loaded PDF, blind to any ledger, and the Auditor agent reviews every line it flags. The expected answers below are the answer key. Check them against your PDF before relying on the score. Labels marked verify are ones not confirmed to exist in your PDF.

Each line is scored on four things: the verdict matches, the amount that counts matches, every article label the agents cited exists in the loaded PDF under that exact nesting, and the article check (an excluded line rests on the exclusion item the key names; a definition cited with an article number, or a label from Appendix D2, the 2027 changes, fails). A line matches the answer key only if all four hold. Lines sent to the controller are counted separately, not as wrong, unless the key expects an escalation.
